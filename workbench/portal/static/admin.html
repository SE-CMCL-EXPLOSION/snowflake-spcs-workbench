Auditor access: read-only. Ask a Workbench admin to make changes.

Grants the Snowflake role WORKBENCH_USER, WORKBENCH_AUDITOR or WORKBENCH_ADMIN. Granting to a role (e.g. one synced from your IdP) gives everyone in it that level.
